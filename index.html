TIPE Capstone Project

Active Magnetic Levitation

Designed and built a closed-loop analog system capable of keeping a metal ball suspended at a fixed point in the air without any physical contact.

What I did: Sized the electromagnet, soldered the power circuit, and tuned a real-time PID controller using a Hall-effect sensor.

What I learnt: Bridging the gap between ideal equations and real hardware noise taught me patience and systematic troubleshooting.

Skills gained: PID tuning, analog electronics, sensor calibration, hardware debugging.
Control & Simulation

Automotive ABS Optimization

Modeled wheel-road slip dynamics during emergency braking to prevent wheel lock-up and reduce stopping distances.

What I did: Simulated and benchmarked 3 control laws in Simulink: a relay controller, a classical PID, and a pseudo-AI optimized PID.

What I learnt: How to objectively compare algorithms and balance fast response time against system stability.

Skills gained: MATLAB/Simulink, non-linear modeling, critical analysis of control methods.
Embedded Robotics

Obstacle-Avoidance Robot Car

Built a small autonomous robotic car capable of moving around an indoor room while detecting and avoiding walls.

What I did: Wired infrared distance sensors and motor drivers, and coded a reactive obstacle-avoidance algorithm in C/Python.

What I learnt: Writing clean, simple code that reacts reliably in real time when sensor readings fluctuate.

Skills gained: Embedded C, microcontroller interfacing, real-time decision logic.

Research Immersion • MIS Laboratory (Amiens)

Shadowed researchers working on robotics testbeds and non-linear control.

↳ Key takeaway: Confirmed my interest in R&D and showed me how academic control theory is tested on real prototypes.

IEEE International Conference (Seoul, 2023)

Attended sessions on next-generation semiconductors and automation in South Korea.

↳ Key takeaway: Sparked my ambition to work in an international tech environment, especially in East Asia.
